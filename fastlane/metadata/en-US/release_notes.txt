PeerDrop 5.5.2 — more reliable first-time pairing, plus everything new in the 5.5 series.

Connection reliability (5.5.2): a set of fixes to pairing and the encrypted-session handshake. The "first time meeting this peer" verification sheet now reliably appears on the very first connection (previously it could stay hidden until you reconnected), the encrypted channel comes up consistently on both local Wi-Fi and relay, and accepting a connection no longer races the transport setup. If pairing ever felt like it needed a second try, this release is for you.

Your pet, everywhere (5.5.0): pets now sync across your devices through iCloud. Hatch an egg on your iPhone and the same companion — same species, same mood, same stats — greets you on your iPad. No setup; it uses the iCloud account you're already signed into.

More companions (5.5.0 + 5.5.1): 25 new species families unlocked, bringing the total to 34 — and 40 more pets gained full walk and idle animations instead of a static sprite.

Compatibility: 5.5.x talks to earlier v5 peers exactly as before. The on-wire protocol version is unchanged.
